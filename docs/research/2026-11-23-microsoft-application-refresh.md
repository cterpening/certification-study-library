## Redis vector search

For a 40 GB searchable index, Enterprise is the eligible clustering policy. The Non-clustered option has a 25 GB limit. Module availability also depends on the Redis version and tier. AI-200 now applies those constraints to the migration exercise and readiness answers 42 and 50.
