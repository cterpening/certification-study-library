# Certification catalog audit — September 17, 2026

The first broad discovery pass attempted all **26 registered vendors**, plus
**Google Education, OpenAI, and Anthropic**. The comparison used 226 seed records,
223 published guides, and the two partner AI references. It found meaningful
coverage gaps and corrected outdated OpenAI/Anthropic credential descriptions.
The [follow-up review](2026-09-17-catalog-follow-up.md) completed enumeration of
the seven initially unresolved catalog scopes and identified October Microsoft
exam transitions plus additional IBM, MongoDB, MOS and ServiceNow candidates.

This is a catalog audit, not a new technical audit of all 223 guides. A catalog
listing establishes a research candidate; availability, exact exam identity,
blueprint, eligibility, and launch date require individual evidence. The priority
findings below received that additional review where stated. Existing breadth
gaps are not automatically newly launched credentials.

## Recurring check and evidence

The configured sweep covers **41 sources** across the 29 vendor/program groups:
37 weekly catalog scopes and four monthly announcement/status scopes. The initial
full attempt returned 34 usable sources, seven manual-review results, and no
network/HTTP errors outside those access limitations. It captured 672 listing
observations. The initial accepted baseline retained **35 successful source observations
and 686 listings**, including the earlier same-day OpenAI Academy capture.
These totals count observations, not distinct or validated certifications.

The seven initially unresolved sources were GitHub, Microsoft, Cisco,
MongoDB, ServiceNow, IBM, and OpenAI Academy. The follow-up completed their scoped
catalog reviews and improved GitHub/Microsoft extraction. The current accepted
baseline has **37 sources and 766 observations**. Four of these seven sources
still require browser review in unattended runs; OpenAI succeeded on retry.
See the [follow-up evidence and limits](2026-09-17-catalog-follow-up.md).
Never convert a blocked result into “no changes.”

Raw comparison results and URL/exam-code matches are retained in
`ADLC_Docs/operations/2026-09-17-certification-discovery.json`; accepted listing
snapshots are in `data/certification-discovery-baseline.json`. Public PDF study
guides were inspected in temporary storage, not copied into the repository.
This audit does not renew seed verification dates or technical guide audits.

The [automation instructions](../AUTOMATION.md) describe local runs, baseline
review, Wednesday checks, and the first-of-month broader review. Schedules need
the workflow on the default branch. Monthly automation fetches known channels
and emits a checklist; official web searches, additional catalog/pagination
review, and final credential classification remain explicit review work.
