## Checks and limits

Three research roles checked exam identity and lifecycle, selected technical documentation, and 59 learning resources. The coordinator compared the evidence to the library's teaching, separated historical and future outlines, and retained prior bytes for dated reviews. This was a source and content refresh. Human review, signed-in training, vendor exam delivery and live tenant or device testing were outside its scope. The [Microsoft review queue](../MICROSOFT-REVIEW-STATUS.md) tracks complete guide reviews separately.

Three fresh-context reviewers examined the six packets against complete guide files, objective hashes, submitted diffs and primary-source evidence. They rechecked corrections across all 56 guides. Their findings included the PL-900 credential notice, the MB-500 exam rename, the AB-250 future-domain count, the Cosmos DB preview boundary and exclusions for Azure SQL backup immutability. The [evidence ledger](2026-11-20-microsoft-refresh-evidence.json) binds their conclusions to the reviewed guide and objective versions.

The initial objective collector checked 54 then-current exams. It reported 38 matches, 15 changes and one extraction error. Navigation text caused the AZ-104 error, and a task mentioning change logs truncated AZ-401. A corrected extraction from the full Microsoft page renderings recovered both sections. All 76 AZ-401 tasks matched the accepted order. Raw downloads were rate-limited, so the reconstructed text is evidence of task content only. [Azure infrastructure comparison](2026-11-20-microsoft-refresh-group-03.md)

For **AZ-104, DP-750 and DP-800**, the published page displays an older dated edition than the library previously captured. **DP-700** presently displays an undated 49-task outline alongside evidence of a dated 54-task October edition. The guides explain exactly where the editions differ and retain the later dated teaching. Microsoft's intent for individual exam deliveries is unresolved.
