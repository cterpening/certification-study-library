# Non-Microsoft certification refresh — Group 14

Completed October 29, 2026 for ten guides selected in the frozen oldest-first queue. Current issuer pages, objective and lifecycle monitors, new learning/product material, and linked-source health were checked. The pre-refresh queue contained 167 guides; 27 remain after this group.

## Exam scope and lifecycle

- **Linux Foundation LFCA and LFCS:** the public LFCA exam page and September 2025 change notice still describe the current six-domain exam; the LFCS page still describes a two-hour performance-based command-line exam. Their captured objective sets and exam contracts remain unchanged. The current kernel.org stable index lists Linux 6.18.59 (October 26) and 6.12.114 (October 21); those are version-maintenance examples, not required exam kernels. Distribution support and backport policies remain authoritative for deployments.
- **MongoDB Associate Atlas Administrator, Data Modeler, and Developer:** current public exam-course URLs resolve to enrollment shells, so the existing captured public outlines remain the objective baseline; no new exam version was inferred. MongoDB’s [August 18, 2026 update](https://www.mongodb.com/company/blog/news/introducing-a-more-connected-flexible-path-to-certifications) aligns the Data Modeler and Atlas Administrator learning paths with Skill Badges and advertises a 50% exam discount after completing the full path. The Data Modeler badges cover modeling, schema patterns, and optimization; the Atlas path covers administration, security, networking, and resiliency.
- **Network+ N10-009:** the current official page continues to identify V9, a 90-minute exam, and an estimated 2027 retirement window without an exact retirement date. Wi-Fi 7 / 802.11be multi-link operation is added as a current troubleshooting example, clearly outside any claimed objective update.
- **NVIDIA NCA-AIIO:** weighted areas remain 38/40/22 and the exam remains 50 questions in 60 minutes. The current page now includes sample questions and a Learning Mode. The October DGX Spark release adds current appliance-management examples but does not alter the certification scope.
- **NVIDIA NCA-GENL:** the public exam description showed no new version or weight change. NVIDIA’s October 15 Nemotron 3.5 Super VL announcement and current NeMo Framework documentation provide new multimodal model/customization material, labeled optional product context.
- **NVIDIA NCP-AIO:** the objective scraper could not extract the refreshed page because its page structure changed, so the current first-party exam page was checked manually. It now presents Install and Deploy 31%, Administration 23%, Workload Management 23%, and Troubleshooting and Optimization 23%, with 30 multiple-choice questions and three live-VM lab challenges in 120 minutes; both exam sections contribute 50% to the pass/fail result. The older linked study-guide PDF still has a different outline and weights. The guide follows the current webpage and keeps that source mismatch visible.
- **Fortinet NSE 4 FortiOS:** FortiOS 7.6 Administrator remains **Available** (50–55 questions, 100 minutes, product version 7.6.0). The current page lists FortiOS 8.0 Administrator as **Coming Soon** (50–60 questions, 100 minutes, product version 8.0.0), even though the release notice had forecast an early-October release. No exact 8.0 launch or final 7.6 delivery date is published. The posted 8.0 outline raises deployment/configuration weighting to 25–35% and adds FortiAI/GenAI CLI, telemetry, FortiSASE identity and GenAI DLP, policy tagging, TLS 1.3 VPN negotiation, AI/ML DNS tunnel detection, FortiGuard Extended SaaS DB, SD-WAN utilization, and quantum-safe IPsec. These remain upcoming-version signals, not 7.6 requirements.

## New product and learning material incorporated

- **Linux operations:** added current upstream stable-kernel release context and a safe maintenance practice loop (version/advisory review, reboot planning, kernel-log and service verification) while keeping distribution-specific support in control.
- **MongoDB:** added Atlas’s October Data Explorer settings and federated-user changes, September Intelligent Workload Management GA, Atlas Infinite preview, shell syntax mode, and current vector-search tuning examples. Atlas Push-Based Log Export stops accepting new configurations on **October 30, 2026** and reaches end of life on **February 4, 2027**; the administrator guide now flags migration to external log sinks. Preview features remain clearly marked.
- **NVIDIA:** added October DGX Spark OS/kernel, cloud-init, and workflow-skill updates; the October Nemotron 3.5 Super VL / NeMo path is current GenAI study context. Product-specific and hardware-specific limits are called out.
- **Fortinet:** captured the newly posted FortiOS 8.0 objective delta and the current 7.6-versus-8.0 availability distinction. Several versioned 7.6 documentation URLs report an 8.0.2 canonical URL while their final page remains 7.6.0; the guide retains version-specific destinations instead of silently replacing them.

## Limits

This batch did not access paid course interiors, sit exams, provision Atlas or Fortinet environments, or execute NVIDIA/Linux labs. Fifteen requests were blocked to automated access; issuer pages and public references were read directly where available. MongoDB course-shell layouts prevented a new machine-readable outline extraction. The NCP-AIO page was manually reviewed and the older PDF discrepancy remains visible. No human review or independent AI audit is claimed. Product availability, preview status, hardware compatibility, cloud region, licensing, and certification booking dates can change; recheck the linked issuer pages before acting.
