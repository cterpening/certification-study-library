## Technical reading and executed examples

Selected [NIST IR 8286A Rev. 1](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ir/8286/a/r1/final) pages support careful estimation, bias review, conditional event trees and simulation. An original four-leaf tree conserves total probability and produces a 2.4% modeled annual loss probability from explicitly conditional inputs, with US$24,000 expected loss for a US$1 million consequence. This is fictional arithmetic, not evidence of actual control-failure rates.

A separate triangular loss model specifies minimum US$20,000, mode US$40,000 and maximum US$100,000. Analytic mean is about US$53,333; probability of exceeding US$60,000 is one-third. An actual 100,000-draw seeded simulation gives about US$53,287 and 33.159%. Comparing simulation with analytic results checks implementation of the chosen model; it does not validate the assumptions or quantify real enterprise risk.

The three-point discussion on PDF page 64 (printed page 53) was also visually inspected. Its sigma formula applied to its stated inputs produces US$7,500, while its prose interval uses US$13,500 around the weighted mean; it also conflates an individual-loss range with confidence for a mean. Those numerical/statistical assertions are not adopted. The complete [NIST statistics handbook page](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda352.htm) supports a separate explanation of repeated-sampling confidence for the population mean. Source authority does not remove the need to check arithmetic and assumptions.

The full [NIST risk-register schema](https://csrc.nist.gov/files/pubs/ir/8286/r1/final/docs/risk_register_schema.json) was captured with an explicit JSON Accept header after an HTML-only follow-up failed. It requires 11 fields and checks response types/uniqueness but does not bound likelihood, enforce nonempty ownership, compute exposure or define local statuses. Actual validation with the existing jsonschema 4.26.0 library demonstrates both accepted and rejected original fixtures. Separate chosen rules flag decision-data gaps without pretending to implement an entire risk policy. The exact schema text/hash and workbook output are preserved in the operation record.

Selected [NIST IR 8286B Update 1](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ir/8286/b/upd1/final) material supports criteria-based prioritization, comparable costs, ownership and explicit handling of deferred treatment. The workbook enumerates all 16 subsets of four fictional actions under a US$65,000 annual budget, rejecting missing dependencies, duplicate IDs and excess cost. Inventory plus identity has the largest projected additive benefit, US$120,000 for US$60,000 cost. A mandatory recovery constraint changes the model's feasible choice to inventory plus recovery, US$115,000 benefit for US$65,000 cost. This models a planning decision; neither selection proves delivery nor authorizes risk acceptance.
