## Learning changes

Four original worked examples explain an application's two tenant instances,
access-review decisions versus effective removal, NSG priority and connection
state, and retention end dates. The guide now includes **seven labs and eighteen
answered checks**: answers were added for the original six questions and twelve
new questions test the expanded distinctions.

The review develops the workload-identity and Microsoft Threat Intelligence
objectives, adds network-segmentation coverage, and distinguishes human versus
software policies, label publication versus application, and vulnerability
recommendations versus incident evidence. Data Explorer terminology and privileged
content permissions supplement the blueprint's Content explorer wording.

Current lifecycle notes cover passkey registration/preference, population-specific
Microsoft SMS/voice delivery retirement, new-subscription Foundational CSPM opt-in,
new-vault Key Vault defaults and the Sentinel portal transition. Updated September
Learn guidance refines the July passkey blog's earlier blanket February deadline.
Five dated checkpoints include shared identity follow-ups for SC-300 and MS-102.

Eight offline checks verified synthetic counts, sets, ordering, retention-year
arithmetic and public course/question sums. No identity, policy, access review,
network, Key Vault, Purview, Sentinel, credential or other tenant action was
executed. All seven labs remain learner exercises/tabletops.
