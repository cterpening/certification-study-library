# Published-exam validation — September 17, 2026

Checked all **223 published guides across 26 vendors** against the public official sources that were accessible. This extends the [catalog review](2026-09-17-catalog-follow-up.md) to individual exams and credential paths. **22 guides received bounded corrections**, and one Salesforce seed URL was repaired.

This is a dated public-source check, not a claim that all guide content is validated. Exact extraction matches, weighted-scope comparisons, public delivery facts, and remaining access gaps are reported separately. A page returning HTTP 200 was not sufficient: browser rendering recovered shell pages, and downloaded PDFs were read as PDFs.

Of the 182 completed automated comparisons, **128 objective extractions match** and 54 differ. Twelve of the 66 combined alerts concern only status. Fourteen alerts include a previously absent status baseline. These are overlapping diagnostic counts, not 66 proven blueprint changes.

The fallbacks cover 17 Palo Alto Networks datasheets (including the Apprentice PDF linked from its page), 10 Splunk PDFs, five Python/JavaScript syllabi, three Salesforce Help blueprints, and two ServiceNow blueprints. Their scope checks compare published domains and weights and inspect the available tasks and exam contract. The Salesforce and ServiceNow browser recovery does not change their HTTP monitor limitations.

All 223 entries have a public identity, exam/credential overview, program announcement, or retirement source inspected. That includes MongoDB's public exam contract even though its detailed scope is gated. Fortinet MSSP is still only announced: its canonical page supplies no exam blueprint or registration contract. Retired SOL-C01 was verified against its transition FAQ, not treated as an active exam.

The machine-readable record is `ADLC_Docs/operations/2026-09-17-exam-validation.json`. It records each guide, before/after guide hashes, comparison hashes, source URLs, retrieval method, findings, and limits. Trusted objective snapshots and historical full-review/AI-audit dates were preserved. Monitor candidates were written to a temporary snapshot copy, so comparison output did not silently replace an accepted baseline.

- **MongoDB:** Developer, Data Modeler and Atlas Administrator public pages confirm 53/75, 75/110 and 70/95 questions/minutes respectively, online proctoring and no formal prerequisite. The linked detailed guides require enrollment or email submission; full scope remains unverified in this pass.
- **Fortinet:** MSSP has no published exam blueprint. Several option exams remain Coming Soon. NSE 8's required electives remain future releases, so the new core-plus-elective path cannot yet be completed. Public partial descriptions were not treated as delivered exams.
- **Vendor inconsistencies:** CPP-22-02 still has weights totaling 107%; PCET/PCAT introductory version text disagrees with their headers. These are recorded, not repaired by inventing vendor facts.
- **Comparison limits:** older paraphrased snapshots differ from current extraction formatting. Some composite Fortinet snapshots cover more than the canonical page, and the NSE 5 SASE adapter currently reads the first, retiring form. Python syllabus extraction omits summary weight tables that were checked separately. The per-exam record preserves these distinctions; semantic reconciliation of every line and any future monitor changes remain separate work.

No private eligibility, appointment inventory, payment flow, paid/enrolled course content, or lab was exercised. No fresh practitioner review or independent technical audit is claimed. Public prerequisites were distinguished from recommended preparation and from the separate requirements to earn a multi-exam credential.
