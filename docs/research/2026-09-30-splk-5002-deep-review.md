The original worksheet uses four synthetic rows, including a replay and a same-name user in another tenant. These are transparent arithmetic predictions, not observed ES results or vendor default thresholds. The [ES 8.0 limitations](https://help.splunk.com/en/splunk-enterprise-security-8/release-notes-and-resources/8.0/splunk-enterprise-security-release-notes/limitations) and [8.5+ pairing matrix](https://help.splunk.com/en/splunk-enterprise-security-8/release-notes-and-resources/8.6/splunk-enterprise-security-release-notes/compatibility-and-regional-availability) support different ES/SOAR deployment combinations. The guide now requires version, topology, region and certificate checks. [SOAR Cloud playbook guidance](https://help.splunk.com/en/splunk-soar/soar-cloud/develop-apps/build-playbooks/introduction-to-splunk-soar-playbooks/use-playbooks-to-automate-analyst-workflows-in-splunk-soar-cloud) says a restart cancels a running playbook without undoing completed changes; the guide calls out that recovery boundary.
