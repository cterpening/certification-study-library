This refresh covers **227 published guides** and **13 inventory-only records** across **26 providers**. The table links every certification to its guide or issuer and to the source review that covers it. 32 guides use the November 20 Microsoft research as their latest substantive packet. The November 23 collection supplies the newer monitoring observations.

- The November Microsoft scope updates include MB-820 and edition-specific guidance for MB-310. CISM teaching follows the effective four-domain outline. Their previous objective snapshots remain available.

## What the checks established

The initial monthly run covered the 225 guides present at its starting revision. It found 98 unchanged objective records, 76 changes to objective text or status, eight extraction errors, 40 manual checks and three retired baselines. Targeted checks then covered the two newly integrated guides and the 18 Microsoft records whose starting observations differed. The closing verification rechecked 14 records, including all eight initial extraction errors. The public English MLA-C01 route and both ServiceNow knowledge bases require manual source checks. The version-specific Python recheck reproduced all three retained objective hashes. The detailed packets explain the changes, source conflicts and fallbacks.

Of 6,321 registered source records covering 6,297 distinct URLs at the starting revision, 5,809 responded successfully, 454 blocked automated access, and 58 returned missing or error outcomes. The follow-up examined all 58. Fifteen recovered on retry, two were readable through an alternate client and others had replacements, narrower sources, historical routes or unresolved access. The scanner also flagged metadata differences in 360 records covering 358 distinct URLs. Every flagged record was classified. The accompanying ledger distinguishes direct checks of provider pages from metadata-only observations and records the resulting guide corrections. A title, redirect or duration change alone cannot establish a course or product revision. The source record includes current guide citations and separate observations for 58 sources added or rerouted during the refresh. [Source rechecks](2026-11-23-source-rechecks.json)

Of 42 discovery sources, 19 matched the previous catalog, 18 changed and five needed manual inspection. The follow-up distinguishes version transitions from broader catalog leads and records provider access limits. [Catalog discovery triage](2026-11-23-discovery-triage.json)

The review used public sources and fresh-context AI review of changed packets. Earlier records identify any separately documented practical checks. This refresh performed no live exam bookings or product labs. Source reachability, a matching digest and a bounded technical check each provide different evidence.
