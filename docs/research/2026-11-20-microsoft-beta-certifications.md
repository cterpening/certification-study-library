# Microsoft beta certifications and announced exams

On November 20, 2026, two Microsoft-issued credentials offered scheduling and carried “beta” in their current titles. AB-330 now has a study guide, and AZ-401 already had one. GitHub maintains a third beta delivered through Microsoft Learn, GH-700. The library also tracks a named Outlook exam for which Microsoft has published only limited details.

## Earlier beta announcements

**SC-730 · Cybersecurity Business Professional.** The credential title still contains “beta.” Microsoft's exam-specific notice says it decided against releasing the certification after its beta. [Microsoft credential notice](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/cybersecurity-business-professional/) · [June decision](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/skills-hub-blog/new-microsoft-certified-cybersecurity-business-professional-certification/4494124)

**AB-210 · Dynamics 365 Sales AI Consultant Associate.** Microsoft's July partner bulletin lists availability from July 9, and the credential title no longer says beta. Its booking page still carries exam-specific beta scoring language. The library retains a provisional beta label pending explicit confirmation of general availability and scoring terms. Candidates can use the existing guide and check their appointment for those terms. [AB-210 guide](../../guides/AB-210-accelerating-sales-pipelines-ai-dynamics-365.md) · [July availability notice](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/announcements/2026-july) · [Credential and scoring notice](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/d365-sales-ai-consultant-associate/)
