## Findings and bounded recommendations

1. **High confidence, affects all ten:** represent current credentials as NSE1/NSE2/NSE3 Cybersecurity plus NSE5/NSE6 under one of four specialist tracks in the post-2026-07-15 program. Keep content anchored to the underlying course/exam objectives, not an assumption that prior FCF/FCA/FCP/FCSS wording remains the current designation. Explain active NSE4 prerequisite for technical levels 5 and 6; avoid saying a single course completion constitutes those credentials. The 2026 ordering guide and Sep 8 requirements FAQ give current evidence.
2. **High confidence, NSE5-SASE:** guide/checklist has a near-term version transition. The 7.6 exam page is explicitly available only through 2026-11-14, and the official Oct 2 release log says the NSE 5 FortiSASE and SD-WAN 26 exam launched Jul 29. Update references/objective map to the 26 exam only after opening and mapping its canonical objectives; preserve a short note that the 7.6 version is nearing final delivery if useful for learners. Don't continue using the 7.6 product-version objective matrix as current beyond the last delivery date.
3. **High confidence, NSE5 Cloud:** new NSE5 FortiAppSec 26 exam was released Aug 27 per Oct 2 official release log; include it as a certification pathway and review its canonical objectives. Track page says FortiADC exam “available in Q3 2026,” while direct page still says Coming soon on Oct 29. Treat availability as unresolved/coming-soon pending a current status check; do not claim it is available.
4. **High confidence, NSE6 Cloud:** NSE6 FortiDDoS 7.2 exam release is dated Aug 14. Track page's FortiMail Workspace 26 exam is labeled “available in Q3 2026,” but direct page still says Coming Soon on Oct 29. Update to distinguish released FortiDDoS from not-yet-available FortiMail Workspace; do not infer that forecast became release.
5. **High confidence, NSE6 Secure Networking:** FortiManager 7.6 and FortiNAC 7.6 released Jul 15 per release log. The page still marks FortiVoice 7.4 and FortiAnalyzer 7.6 “available in Q3”; Oct 2 notice calls their release upcoming in October. Confirm each live exam status and objective page before changing their currentness.
6. **Medium confidence / official source inconsistency, NSE6 SASE:** level page associates FortiClient EMS, FortiEDR, and FortiDLP exams with the SASE track, while its prose defines this as endpoint protection/data security and not FortiSASE access. Preserve exact exam list and state track mapping explicitly; flag the description copy mismatch rather than silently reconciling it.
7. **NSE1-3:** credential pages are current and have no apparent lifecycle/retirement alert. Add/prioritize updated fundamentals/course objectives only if the live course content differs materially from the Sept 2 baseline. The 2026 helpdesk explicitly changed/reorganized fundamental training; do not assume course content changes from the structural notice alone.
