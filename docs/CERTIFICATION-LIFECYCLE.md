For Microsoft Learn study guides, the skills date is transcribed from the exact English 'Skills measured as of' heading. It does not by itself prove that the preceding outline changed on that day. A beta release is different from general availability. A rename does not by itself create a new exam or replacement. Language-specific dates are shown with their language code; do not apply them to every exam delivery. A catalog check date describes an inventory pass, not a fresh individual lifecycle check.
