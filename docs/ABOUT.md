# About the Certification Study Library

The Certification Study Library is an independent, vendor-neutral learning project maintained by [Chris Terpening (`cterpening`)](https://github.com/cterpening). It turns public certification objectives and product documentation into original, cited study guides with explanations, decision guidance, labs, readiness checks, and curated learning options.

The project began with GitHub and Microsoft certifications and now also covers HashiCorp credentials. Its goal is not to collect the most pages or prescribe one learning style. It is to make each published objective easier to understand, apply, verify, and revisit as products and blueprints change.

## Current maturity

The site is a working public library under active expansion. Published guides have complete objective maps and have passed the repository's sources-and-objectives quality gate. The catalog, source records, objective snapshots, automated checks, and deployment workflow are operational.

Independent practitioner review is still in progress. GH-300, AZ-104, and Terraform Associate (004) are the first flagship guides for which the project is seeking complete external review. A guide must not be presented as **Community reviewed** until that review is recorded.

## What the review labels mean

| Displayed state | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| AI-generated draft | Public-source synthesis exists | Complete source, objective, or human review |
| Sources + objectives checked — human review pending | Objective coverage, citations, volatility labels, tracked links, and exam-integrity checks passed for the dated baseline | Independent human endorsement or a guarantee that every explanation is correct |
| Community reviewed | A contributor reviewed the complete guide in addition to the sources-and-objectives gate | Vendor endorsement or permanent currency |
| Review required | A monitored source changed after the last accepted baseline | That the guide is unusable; the changed area needs inspection |
| Retired | The credential is no longer active | That every underlying product concept is obsolete |

The linked official vendor blueprint remains authoritative in every state. The detailed [validation records](SOURCE-VALIDATION.md) show exactly what the project checked, and the [source-quality policy](SOURCE-QUALITY.md) explains which evidence is acceptable.

## Independence and integrity

This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by a certification vendor or training provider. It does not accept recalled live questions, exam dumps, copied paid training, confidential material, employer data, or customer data. Commercial and community learning resources can be evaluated and linked from their public pages, but they do not define technical truth or exam scope.

Read the [content and exam-integrity policy](CONTENT-POLICY.md) for the full boundary and the [source-quality policy](SOURCE-QUALITY.md) for the operating principles.

## How the project is maintained

- Official objective pages are compared with dated snapshots on a weekly schedule.
- Approved public sources are monitored for reachability and meaningful metadata changes.
- Detected changes create review work; automation does not silently rewrite study advice.
- The website is generated from vendor, exam, collection, source, and review catalogs and must pass repository and site validation before deployment.
- Corrections are welcome through the [public issue forms](https://github.com/cterpening/certification-study-library/issues/new/choose).
