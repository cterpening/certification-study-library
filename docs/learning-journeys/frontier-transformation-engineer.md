# Frontier Transformation Engineer

> **Current public baseline:** The [LevelUp Frontier Transformation Engineer journey](https://skillupwithlevelup.com/frontier) displayed **11 courses, approximately 86 hours, and three milestones** when checked September 1, 2026. The landing page is public; enrollment, course content, assessments, progress, and reporting require eligible partner access. Verify the live page because requirements and account-linking instructions can change.

Frontier Transformation Engineer is best represented here as a **journey page that points to three certification guides**, not as an eighth Microsoft certification or a new vendor. The LevelUp badge composes existing Microsoft/GitHub credentials with project-readiness and Hypervelocity Engineering requirements.

!!! info "What this page does"
    This page explains the publicly visible sequence, links the three certifications to this library, and identifies the partner-only boundary. It does not reproduce LevelUp course lessons, labs, assessments, answers, screenshots, or learner data.

## Journey at a glance

| Program element | Publicly displayed requirement |
|---|---|
| Intended audience | Solution engineers, AI engineers, cloud engineers, solution architects, technical consultants, and other technical professionals delivering AI-powered solutions |
| Access | LevelUp account created with a company email associated with an eligible Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program organization |
| Structure | Three milestones: certifications; Project Ready + assessments; Frontier CoE & Hypervelocity Engineering |
| Full learning estimate | Approximately 86 hours across 11 listed courses; actual preparation depends on prior experience |
| Badge completion | Required credentials and assessments must be validated in LevelUp; certification recognition can take two to three weeks |
| Reporting | Authorized partner administrators can view organizational enrollment, progress, assessment status, and badge attainment under the program's current permissions |

The 86-hour figure is internally consistent with the visible course estimates: 30 hours of optional certification preparation, 51 hours of Project Ready preparation, and 4.5 hours across the three Frontier CoE/Hypervelocity/industry items, totaling 85.5 hours before individual practice or exam time.

LevelUp labels the preparation courses optional and the certification status required. Use the official blueprint as scope, this library for public study, and the gated course when your partner access makes it useful.

| **AI-103: Develop AI apps and agents on Azure** | 10 hours | [AI-103 study guide](../../guides/AI-103-developing-ai-apps-and-agents-on-azure.md) | Build, evaluate, secure, deploy, and operate Foundry-based generative AI applications and agents. |

### AI-102 substitution boundary

The current LevelUp page says an **already-held active AI-102 certification** is accepted in place of AI-103 for this badge. That is a Frontier badge rule, not a statement that the retired AI-102 exam remains available, that AI-102 and AI-103 have identical scope, or that one credential automatically converts into the other. New learners should plan against AI-103 unless LevelUp shows a different current rule.

> **Related item:** The trio intentionally spans individual developer productivity, implementation of AI applications/agents, and business-solution architecture. Study them as three altitudes of one delivery system rather than three unrelated exams.

The current journey lists five preparation modules. LevelUp describes preparation as optional while requiring the associated assessments at a minimum **80%** score. The FAQ says assessments may be retaken up to ten times and that included labs are recommended or may be required for specific modules.

## Milestone 3 — Frontier CoE and Hypervelocity Engineering

The page currently shows one required completion in this milestone:

- **Hypervelocity Engineering Framework** — 1.5 hours of listed preparation and a required assessment with an 80% minimum.

It also lists **Create AI Center of Excellence** (2 hours) and **Industry Expertise for building AI agents** (1 hour) as optional. These broaden organizational operating-model and industry context but do not replace the required Hypervelocity assessment under the September 1 baseline.

> **Related item:** Certifications establish individual knowledge. Project-readiness assessments exercise delivery context. A CoE and engineering framework address repeatability across teams. The badge combines all three evidence types.

## Account and validation setup

Complete the identity work early so passed credentials can be recognized correctly:

1. Create the LevelUp account with your eligible partner **work email**. The current page says personal email domains are not supported for LevelUp registration.
2. Keep a personal-email Microsoft Learn profile as the durable profile, then link the relevant work-email and any prior Learn profiles so credential history is consolidated.
3. Link that Microsoft Learn profile in Partner Center using the work account. The current instructions say Partner Center requires at least one completed exam, certification, or Applied Skill before it permits the link.
4. Confirm the linked profile appears in Partner Center, then enroll and monitor progress in LevelUp.
5. Allow the displayed two-to-three-week synchronization window after enrollment and an exam pass before escalating a missing certification status.

Use the current LevelUp walkthrough and your partner administrator's instructions as authority. Do not publish learner identifiers, screenshots, scores, profile-link details, or organizational reports in this repository.

## Suggested study order

There is no single mandatory order for every experienced learner, but this sequence builds from individual workflow to implementation to architecture:

1. **GH-300** — establish effective, secure AI-assisted development habits.
2. **AI-103** — build and operate the applications and agents those workflows support.
3. **AB-100** — connect technical implementation to enterprise process, adoption, governance, and value.
4. **Project Ready assessments** — use LevelUp's gap results to revisit the relevant guide sections and hands-on work.
5. **Hypervelocity Engineering** — close the required delivery-framework milestone after the technical foundations are concrete.

If your role begins in architecture, preview AB-100 first to understand the destination, then return to GH-300 and AI-103 for implementation evidence before completing AB-100 in depth.
