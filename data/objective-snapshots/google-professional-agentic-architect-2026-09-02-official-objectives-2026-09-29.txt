The Professional Agentic Architect certification assesses your
ability to:
Build agents using low-code tools
Use coding agents for application development
Develop custom agents
Evaluate and deploy agentic workflows
Secure and govern agentic workflows
To validate these real-world capabilities, the certification will consist of two
parts:
A proctored multiple-choice exam to assess conceptual knowledge, system
design choices, and architectural standards delivered by Pearson
Hands-on labs to validate hands-on execution and coding ability taken in
Google Skills
