NSE 6 in Cloud Security
Official certification page checked: 2026-09-02
https://training.fortinet.com/local/staticpage/view.php?page=nse_6_cloud_security

Description
The certification validates the ability to deploy, manage, and monitor Fortinet advanced application security products to secure public and private cloud applications. The page says option exams cover day-to-day tasks related to Fortinet application security devices and VMs.

Program requirements
- Hold the NSE 4 FortiOS certification.
- Pass one proctored NSE 6 Cloud Security exam within 2 years.
- Certification is active for 2 years from the second exam.

Published option exams
- Fortinet NSE 6 - FortiCNAPP Analyst
- Fortinet NSE 6 - FortiMail Administrator
- Fortinet NSE 6 - FortiDDoS Administrator
- Fortinet NSE 6 - FortiMail WorkSpace Security Administrator (marked available in Q3 2026; linked page still says Coming soon on 2026-09-02)

FortiCNAPP Analyst published domains
- FortiCNAPP fundamentals (25–35%)
- End-to-end risk management (35–45%)
- Threat detection and response (25–35%)
- Product baseline: FortiCNAPP 26

FortiMail Administrator published objective groups
- Initial deployment and basic configuration
- Email flow and authentication
- Email security
- Encryption
- Server mode and transparent mode
- Product baseline: FortiMail 7.4

FortiDDoS Administrator published domains
- DDoS fundamentals (20–30%)
- Deployment, configuration, and baselining (25–35%)
- Protection and mitigation controls (25–35%)
- Monitoring, logging, and analysis (10–20%)
- Product baseline: FortiDDoS 7.2
