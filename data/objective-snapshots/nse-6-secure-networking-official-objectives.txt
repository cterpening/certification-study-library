NSE 6 in Secure Networking
Official certification page checked: 2026-09-02
https://training.fortinet.com/local/staticpage/view.php?page=nse_6_secure_networking

Description
The certification validates the ability to deploy, manage, and monitor Fortinet advanced network security products to secure networks and applications.

Program requirements
- Hold the NSE 4 FortiOS certification.
- Pass one proctored NSE 6 Secure Networking exam within 2 years.
- Certification is active for 2 years from the second exam.

Published option exams
- Fortinet NSE 6 - FortiManager Administrator
- Fortinet NSE 6 - FortiNAC Administrator
- Fortinet NSE 6 - FortiVoice Administrator (marked available in Q3 2026; linked page still says Coming soon on 2026-09-02)
- Fortinet NSE 6 - FortiAnalyzer Administrator (marked available in Q3 2026; linked page still says Coming soon on 2026-09-02)
