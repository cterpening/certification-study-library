Fortinet NSE 5 in Security Operations objective snapshot
Track source: https://training.fortinet.com/local/staticpage/view.php?page=nse_5_security_operations
Exam source: https://training.fortinet.com/local/staticpage/view.php?page=fortianalyzer_analyst_exam
Verified: 2026-09-02

Certification requirement
- Hold active NSE 4 FortiOS.
- Pass one proctored NSE 5 Security Operations exam within 2 years while NSE 4 is active.
- Certification is active for 2 years from completion of the second requirement.

Current listed exam
- Fortinet NSE 5 - FortiAnalyzer 7.6 Analyst
- Status: Available
- Time: 65 minutes
- Questions: 30-35
- Languages: English and Japanese
- Product: FortiAnalyzer 7.6

Published topic groups (no weights published)
1. Features and concepts
   - Explain Fabric integration and log collection.
   - Explain log data flow, normalization, and parsing.
   - Explain SOC features on FortiAnalyzer.
2. Log Analysis
   - Analyze logs, events, and incidents.
   - Analyze FortiView dashboards and widgets.
   - Diagnose and troubleshoot report generation issues.
3. SOC operation and automation
   - Configure/manage events and event handlers.
   - Configure incidents and indicators.
   - Configure playbooks and Fabric automation.
   - Troubleshoot playbook and Fabric automation issues.
4. Reports
   - Explain reports, charts, and datasets.
   - Configure reports.
   - Troubleshoot report generation.

Boundary note
- The track currently lists only FortiAnalyzer Analyst. Do not infer eligibility from retired FortiSandbox or exams moved to another NSE level.
