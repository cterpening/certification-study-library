Requirements Gathering: 10%
Identify application operations and performance priorities that drive the model.
Identify the business, data-source, governance, and technical information required before modeling.

Entities: 13%
Identify persisted entities, attributes, types, and domain groupings.
Identify domain ownership and where duplication and synchronization may be required.

Relationships: 8.5%
Identify relationships, cardinality, skew, strong entities, weak entities, and identifying keys.

Workload/Usage: 10%
Describe reads, writes, updates, deletes, aggregations, frequency, priority, latency, volume, and growth.
Use workload evidence to identify operations that require model optimization.

Data Model Design: 28%
Select embedding or referencing for entity relationships and access patterns.
Apply appropriate schema design patterns and identify anti-patterns.
Evaluate duplication, consistency, atomicity, document growth, and application complexity tradeoffs.

Modeling for Technical Requirements: 10%
Adapt the model for validation, data types, lifecycle, transactions, analytics, sharding, and other stated technical constraints.

Indexing: 13%
Select indexes from query predicates, sort, range, projection, selectivity, and workload needs.
Interpret explain evidence and balance read benefit against storage and write cost.

Monitoring and Evolving Data Models: 7.5%
Use workload and performance evidence to detect model problems.
Plan compatible schema evolution, migrations, versioning, validation changes, and rollback.
