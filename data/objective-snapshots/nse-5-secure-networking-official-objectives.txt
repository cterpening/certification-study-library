Fortinet NSE 5 in Secure Networking certification-path snapshot
Source: https://training.fortinet.com/local/staticpage/view.php?page=nse_5_secure_networking
Verified: 2026-10-28

Certification requirement
- Hold an active NSE 4 FortiOS certification.
- Pass one proctored NSE 5 Secure Networking exam within 2 years while NSE 4 is active.
- Certification is valid for 2 years from the most recent requirement.

Current listed exam routes
1. Fortinet NSE 5 - FortiSwitch Administrator
   - Current page: FortiSwitch 7.6 Administrator
   - Status: Available
2. Fortinet NSE 5 - Secure Wireless LAN Administrator
   - Current page: Secure Wireless LAN 7.6 Administrator
   - Status: Available
   - Version 7.4 last delivery: 2026-08-31
3. Fortinet NSE 5 - SD-WAN Core Administrator
   - Current page: SD-WAN 7.6 Core Administrator
   - Status: Available
   - Time: 60-70 minutes; questions: 30-35; language: English
   - SD-WAN Architecture and Concept (15-25%)
   - SD-WAN Traffic Control, Rules and Routing (35-45%)
   - Centralized Management, visibility and troubleshooting (35-45%)

Boundary note
- This track is not a single combined exam. Fortinet publishes separate topics for each available exam; these SD-WAN weights do not apply to the other routes.
