Block 1: Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals — 5 items, 14.0%
1.1 AI, agents, environment, inference, narrow/general AI, system components/applications. 1.2 ML, DL, NLP, CV, robotics and generative AI. 1.3 training/inference, features/labels, feedback and data quality. 1.4 capabilities/limitations, hallucinations, robustness and suitability. 1.5 problem/data/objectives/stakeholders/success metrics.

Block 2: Machine Learning Fundamentals — 6 items, 16.5%
2.1 Supervised/unsupervised/reinforcement learning. 2.2 ML workflow, train/test split, preprocessing and data issues (official title incorrectly says “Levels of Testing”). 2.3 Linear models, trees, k-NN, k-means and Naive Bayes. 2.4 Rule/distance/grouping logic in pure Python. 2.5 Accuracy, precision, recall, confusion matrix, over/underfitting.

Block 3: Data Handling, Analysis, and Visualization — 6 items, 16.5%
3.1 CSV/text/JSON, collections, nested data, cleaning/conversion. 3.2 Statistics, grouping, sorting and patterns. 3.3 Vectors, Euclidean/Manhattan distance, similarity, normalization/features. 3.4 Basic Pandas, filter/select/restructure, feature selection/extraction. 3.5 Matplotlib line/bar/histogram and interpretation. 3.6 Data-quality/model impacts.

Block 4: Neural Networks, Deep Learning, and Generative AI — 8 items, 22.5%
4.1 Neurons, weights, bias, layers, activation, feedforward/backpropagation. 4.2 Classical ML versus DL. 4.3 NLP tokens/sequences/embeddings/tasks/context. 4.4 CV arrays/channels/CNN/tasks. 4.5 Generative AI/LLMs/modalities/limitations. 4.6 Prompt context/task/refinement/injection/verification. 4.7 Pre-trained models, transfer learning, training/inference/deployment.

Block 5: Responsible AI, Ethics, and Critical Thinking — 6 items, 16.5%
5.1 Bias, fairness, privacy, hallucination/misuse. 5.2 Sensitive data, manipulation/bypass, minimization/terms/escalation. 5.3 Social/workforce/economic impacts. 5.4 Fairness/transparency/accountability/explainability/human oversight. 5.5 Verify outputs and override automation appropriately.

Block 6: AI Projects, Collaboration, and Communication — 5 items, 14.0%
6.1 Suitable opportunities, constraints and feasibility. 6.2 Build/use/maintenance/data/compute cost. 6.3 Problem/data/logic/test/evaluate/iterate/document. 6.4 Roles, feedback and ethics collaboration. 6.5 Audience-aware evidence/visualization and limitations.
