Captured: 2026-09-02
Credential: Splunk Certified Cybersecurity Defense Architect (SPLK-5003)
Exam contract: 67 questions; expert level; 75 total minutes, including 3 minutes for the exam agreement
Experience statement: blueprint says candidates often have 5–7 years of relevant experience
Prerequisite exams: none

1.0 Advanced Threat Intelligence and Analysis — 5%
1.1 Develop/implement customized open-source and commercial threat-intelligence strategies
1.2 Integrate the full intelligence lifecycle into security operations
1.3 Integrate intelligence-informed adversary emulation and threat modeling
2.0 Security Data Management — 20%
2.1 Develop/implement integration strategies for data-driven security operations
2.2 Identify critical security/non-security data sources
2.3 Identify high-value/high-signal/high-noise sources and use-case support
2.4 Monitor environments needing nonstandard/out-of-band sensors, including legacy and OT/ICS
2.5 Develop lifecycle strategy: retention, tiers, summarization, residency, access
2.6 Explain normalization such as CIM and CEF
2.7 Implement analytics beyond traditional SIEM, including data science, ML, behavior, AI
2.8 Explain scaling through data mesh/lakes, message bus/routing, federated search
3.0 Advanced Incident Response and Management — 10%
3.1 Align incident response with incident/change management and ITSM/ITIL
3.2 Manage, coordinate, communicate large-scale incidents
3.3 Support forensic investigations with appropriate technology/process
4.0 Advanced Automation and Orchestration — 10%
4.1 Explain architectural enablers/constraints for orchestration
4.2 Develop complex cross-platform security workflows
4.3 Explain autonomous-SOC benefits and development strategies/processes/technology
4.4 Leverage AI/ML for automated detection/response
5.0 Scaling Cybersecurity Defenses and DevSecOps — 15%
5.1 Develop scalable agile/DevOps defense, including detection as code
5.2 Integrate sensors/controls into DevOps workflows
5.3 Create/use an SBOM in defenses
5.4 Describe CI/CD for security-data management/engineering
5.5 Develop patterns, architecture blueprints, and paved roads
5.6 Explain how application/infrastructure architectures support defense
6.0 Governance, Risk, and Compliance — 10%
6.1 Explain influence of governmental directives/guidance such as NIST CSF
6.2 Explain GDPR impacts on logging, sovereignty, residency
6.3 Explain effects of PCI, HIPAA, OT/ICS and other frameworks
6.4 Define security-control operating cost and risk offset
6.5 Integrate controls with GRC and risk management
7.0 Measuring and Improving Security Program Effectiveness — 15%
7.1 Define/measure/report effectiveness metrics
7.2 Relate risk tolerance to metrics
7.3 Apply continuous process improvement
7.4 Continually test controls and remediate gaps
8.0 Security Capability Selection, Placement, Configuration — 15%
8.1 Identify prevention/detection/response/recovery coverage
8.2 Mitigate gaps via architecture, configuration, or process
8.3 Influence priorities/budgets using business-aligned capability needs
8.4 Select technology aligned to business, landscape, and controls
8.5 Define implementation strategies
8.6 Select/deploy resilient solutions aligned to business/operations
