Exam MB-310: Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance Functional Consultant
Official objective baseline: Skills measured as of August 14, 2026
Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/mb-310
Captured: 2026-09-01

Audience profile

Candidates analyze business requirements and translate them into financial
processes and configurations in Dynamics 365 Finance. They understand
accounting principles and financial operations and collaborate with architects,
developers, administrators, and business stakeholders.

Skills at a glance

Implement financial management (35–40%)
Implement accounts receivable, credit, collections, and subscription billing (15–20%)
Implement and manage accounts payable and expenses (10–15%)
Implement budgeting (10–15%)
Manage fixed assets (10–15%)

Implement financial management (35–40%)

Design and configure chart of accounts and financial dimensions
- Configure financial dimensions
- Configure chart of accounts and main accounts
- Configure main account categories, ledger account aliases, and balance control accounts
- Configure legal entity overrides for financial dimensions and main accounts
- Create and configure account structures and advanced rules
- Configure financial tags
- Configure derived dimensions and dimension default templates

Configure ledgers and currencies
- Configure exchange rate types and exchange rates
- Configure currency revaluation posting profiles
- Configure accounting and reporting currencies
- Configure a ledger and fiscal calendar
- Configure posting layers
- Configure ledger allocations

Implement and manage journals
- Configure journal names and voucher numbering
- Configure journal controls and posting restriction rules
- Configure periodic journals
- Configure intercompany accounting
- Create templates and edit journals in Microsoft Excel
- Configure reversals
- Create, validate, and post journals manually and by using Excel
- Configure batch processing for journals

Implement and manage cash and bank
- Configure bank groups and bank accounts
- Configure payment formats
- Configure and process manual and advanced bank reconciliation
- Configure cash flow forecasts, including automation
- Perform bank foreign currency revaluation
- Configure shared payments
- Configure customer and vendor netting

Perform periodic processes
- Configure financial close and end-of-year processes, accounts, reports, and templates
- Perform financial consolidation and elimination
- Configure financial period workspaces and closing schedules
- Configure a legal entity for Consolidation online
- Configure and perform ledger settlements

Configure and process tax
- Configure sales tax groups, item sales tax groups, codes, settlement periods, authorities, and exemptions
- Configure tax posting groups
- Settle and post sales tax
- Configure withholding tax groups, codes, and authorities
- Calculate and adjust sales tax

Implement accounts receivable, credit, collections, and subscription billing (15–20%)

Implement accounts receivable
- Configure customer groups and shared customers
- Create free text and recurring invoices
- Process sales order invoices, credit memos, and prepayments
- Configure payment methods and charges
- Configure customer posting profiles
- Process customer payments
- Perform accounts receivable foreign currency revaluation
- Configure billing classifications and customer bank accounts
- Process intercompany customer invoices

Manage credit and collections
- Configure credit-management and collections processes and activities
- Identify and process delinquent customers
- Configure and process collection letters
- Configure aging definitions and review aged balances
- Configure and process credit holds
- Configure and process write-offs and interest notes

Configure subscription billing
- Generate sales documents by using billing schedules
- Manage billing schedules including price updates, holds, and termination
- Configure contract billing groups, item groups, and milestones
- Configure revenue deferrals and recognition including charges
- Configure expense deferrals and recognition
- Manage recognition schedules
- Configure deferral defaults and items deferred by default

Implement and manage accounts payable and expenses (10–15%)

Implement and manage accounts payable
- Configure invoice validation and matching
- Configure vendor groups, shared vendors, and posting profiles
- Process invoices and credit memos with and without purchase orders, including prepayments
- Configure payment methods and accounts payable charges
- Configure vendor bank accounts
- Perform accounts payable foreign currency revaluation
- Process vendor invoice journals and recurring vendor invoices
- Process vendor and centralized payments and prepayments
- Process intercompany vendor invoices

Implement and manage expenses
- Configure expense categories, per diem, mileage, intercompany, and personal expenses
- Create and manage expense reports and policies

Implement budgeting (10–15%)

Configure and manage basic budgeting
- Configure budget models, codes, allocation terms, and transfer rules
- Create and process budget register entries
- Review budget versus actual

Configure and manage budget control
- Configure parameters, funds-availability calculation, rules, groups, and over-budget permissions
- Validate budget control on transactions

Configure and manage budget planning
- Configure budget plans, scenarios, and allocations
- Configure a budget planning process and organization hierarchy

Manage fixed assets (10–15%)

Implement and manage fixed assets
- Configure fixed asset groups and attributes
- Configure posting profiles
- Configure books and derived books
- Configure depreciation
- Create fixed assets
- Map groups and assets to books

Process fixed asset transactions
- Dispose of assets by using free text invoices
- Acquire assets by using purchase orders, journals, inventory, and projects
- Create asset budgets and transfer them to budgeting
- Run depreciation for multiple companies
- Split, reclassify, transfer, dispose of, and otherwise transact assets by journal

Change log summary

The August 14, 2026 comparison decreases the financial-management weight,
substantially revises ledgers/currencies, journals, periodic processes,
receivables, subscription billing, payables, and fixed-asset transactions,
removes cost management, and adds budget planning.
