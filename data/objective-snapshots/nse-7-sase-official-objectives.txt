Fortinet NSE 7 - FortiSASE 26 Architect objective snapshot
Source: https://training.fortinet.com/local/staticpage/view.php?page=fortisase_architect_exam
Verified: 2026-09-02

1. SD-WAN architecture and deployment (10–20%)
1.1 Design enterprise SD-WAN architectures
1.1.1 Components/use cases, ZTP, blueprints/CSV, multiregion/large topology, MSSP and VRF-aware overlays
1.2 Deploy SD-WAN infrastructure
1.2.1 DIA topology/settings, members/zones, underlay/overlay links, dual hubs and large-deployment settings
1.3 Implement SD-WAN performance controls
1.3.1 SLA targets, active/passive monitoring, member status/actions, advanced settings, ICMP SLA information, FortiManager monitoring and FortiAnalyzer analytics

2. SD-WAN traffic control and IPsec (20–30%)
2.1 Design SD-WAN rules
2.1.1 Lookup, local-out/implicit rules, strategies/criteria, application learning, Internet services, preferred member and monitoring
2.2 Configure SD-WAN routing
2.2.1 Route/policy lookup, member/zone/probe routes, sessions/flags/reevaluation, SNAT changes, BGP/self-healing and dual-hub routing
2.3 Deploy advanced IPsec for SD-WAN
2.3.1 Overlay design, BGP per overlay/on loopback, additional paths, stickiness, scalable hub-and-spoke, overlay-as-a-service and ADVPN

3. FortiSASE architecture and integration (15–25%)
3.1 Evaluate FortiSASE components in advanced scenarios
3.1.1 Provisioning/MSSP/licensing/FortiFlex; SIA agent/agentless/edge; FortiExtender/FortiBranch/FortiAP; branch on-ramp; private proxy; SPA/SSA; dedicated IP; API; POP; residency/sovereignty
3.2 Integrate FortiSASE into hybrid networks
3.2.1 Branch/remote users, edge devices, SD-WAN on-ramp, FortiSASE spoke and FortiAnalyzer forwarding/analytics
3.3 Integrate SD-WAN and FortiSASE workflows
3.3.1 Site-based users, SPA using SD-WAN, single/dual hub and FortiSASE-as-spoke use cases

4. FortiSASE deployment and secure access (20–30%)
4.1 Deploy access and onboarding
4.1.1 SIA agent/agentless/edge, SPA, SAML administration, user sources, installers and dashboards/tools
4.2 Configure endpoint posture and access control
4.2.1 Endpoint profiles, posture tags, upgrades/management, FortiClient, DEM, on/off-net, lockdown, bypass, compliance rules and certificate-based HTTPS proxy
4.3 Deploy cloud-delivered secure services
4.3.1 SPA FortiGate, Security Fabric, SaaS/SPA monitoring, NGFW SPA, security policy, agentless ZTNA and POP/service monitoring

5. Centralized management, visibility and troubleshooting (10–20%)
5.1 Centrally manage FortiSASE and SD-WAN
5.1.1 FortiManager SD-WAN/FortiSASE integration, FortiAnalyzer forwarding/analytics, SOCaaS and FortiGuard forensic analysis
5.2 Analyze traffic and security logs
5.2.1 FortiClient diagnostics, SD-WAN logs, analytics and reports
5.3 Troubleshoot connectivity and policy behavior
5.3.1 SD-WAN commands/state, tunnel connectivity/performance, SPA, FortiClient diagnostics and endpoint captures
