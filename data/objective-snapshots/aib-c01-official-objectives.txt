AWS Certified AI Business Strategist (AIB-C01)
The exam also validates a candidate's ability to complete the following tasks:
Apply foundational AI concepts and terminology to business contexts.
Select appropriate AI solution types based on business requirements and constraints.
Use generative AI (GenAI) techniques, including prompt engineering and model adaptation, to achieve business outcomes.
Develop and prioritize AI strategies that align with organizational objectives.
Measure AI business value using key performance indicators (KPIs), return on investment (ROI) frameworks, and baseline metrics.
Position AI initiatives for competitive advantage and business model transformation.
Apply responsible AI principles when making business decisions that require tradeoffs.
Establish governance structures and ensure regulatory compliance for AI initiatives.
Identify enterprise AI risks and direct appropriate mitigation strategies.
Assess organizational AI readiness and maturity across people, processes, technology, and governance.
Evaluate data and infrastructure foundations required to support AI initiatives.
Lead enterprise-wide change management and build AI-ready workforce capabilities.
Scale AI initiatives from pilots to enterprise-wide deployments by using iterative approaches.
