The Professional Agentic Architect certification assesses your
ability to:
Build agents using low-code tools
Use coding agents for application development
Develop custom agents
Evaluate and deploy agentic workflows
Secure and govern agentic workflows
To validate these real-world capabilities, the certification will consist of
two parts:
Part 1: A proctored multiple-choice exam to assess conceptual
knowledge, system design choices, and architectural standards
delivered by Pearson
Part 2: If you pass the exam, you will receive instructions in CM
Connect to take the distinct PAA hands-on labs in Google Skills to
validate your execution and coding ability
Results of the beta exam will be released in CM Connect at the end of
October. Candidates who pass will receive a notification to complete the
required PAA labs by December 31, 2026.
Click "View FAQs" below for more information about the PAA beta exam.
