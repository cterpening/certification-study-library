Fortinet NSE 5 in SASE objective snapshot
Track source: https://training.fortinet.com/local/staticpage/view.php?page=nse_5_sase
Exam source: https://training.fortinet.com/local/staticpage/view.php?page=fortisase_and_sd-wan_core_administrator_exam
Verified: 2026-09-02

Certification requirement
- Hold active NSE 4 FortiOS.
- Pass Fortinet NSE 5 - FortiSASE and SD-WAN Core Administrator within 2 years.
- Certification is active for 2 years from the NSE 5 exam date.

Current exam baseline
- Fortinet NSE 5 - FortiSASE and SD-WAN 26 Core Administrator
- Time: 60-70 minutes
- Questions: 30-35
- Language: English
- Products: FortiSASE 26, FortiOS 7.6, FortiClient 7.0, FortiAuthenticator 6.5, FortiManager 7.6

Published domains and ranges
1. Decentralized SD-WAN (20-30%)
   - Implement a basic SD-WAN setup.
   - Configure members and zones.
   - Configure performance SLAs.
2. Rules and routing (15-25%)
   - Configure SD-WAN rules.
   - Configure SD-WAN routing.
3. SASE deployment (20-30%)
   - Configure administration settings.
   - Use available user-onboarding methods.
   - Describe FortiSASE integration with SD-WAN.
4. Secure internet access and secure SaaS access (15-25%)
   - Implement security profiles for content inspection.
   - Deploy compliance rules to managed endpoints.
5. Analytics (15-25%)
   - Analyze SD-WAN logs for rule/session behavior.
   - Describe FortiSASE logs used to identify potential threats.
   - Explain reports used to identify user traffic and security issues.

Announced change
- FortiSASE and SD-WAN 7.6 Core Administrator is available only until 2026-11-14 and uses FortiSASE 25.
