NSE 6 in Security Operations
Official certification page checked: 2026-09-02
https://training.fortinet.com/local/staticpage/view.php?page=nse_6_security_operations

Description
The certification validates the ability to deploy, manage, and monitor advanced Fortinet security operations products to secure networks and applications. The page says option exams cover day-to-day tasks related to advanced security operations devices.

Program requirements
- Hold the NSE 4 FortiOS certification.
- Pass one proctored NSE 6 Security Operations exam within 2 years.
- Certification is active for 2 years from the second exam.

Published option exams
- Fortinet NSE 6 - FortiSIEM Analyst
- Fortinet NSE 6 - FortiNDR Cloud Analyst
- Fortinet NSE 6 - FortiSOAR Analyst
- Fortinet NSE 6 - FortiRecon Analyst (pathway says available in August 2026; linked page still says Coming soon on 2026-09-02)
- Fortinet NSE 6 - FortiDeceptor Administrator (pathway says available in August 2026; linked page still says Coming soon on 2026-09-02)

FortiSIEM Analyst published objective groups
- Analytics
- Rules and subpatterns
- Incidents, notifications, and remediation
- ML, UEBA, and ZTNA
- Product baseline: FortiSIEM 7.4

FortiNDR Cloud Analyst published domains
- Architecture and system settings (15–25%)
- Events and queries (25–35%)
- Detection (15–25%)
- Investigations and integrations (20–30%)
- Product baseline: FortiNDR Cloud 26

FortiSOAR Analyst published domains
- FortiSOAR architecture and data models (20–30%)
- Playbook Development (25–35%)
- Incident Handling (5–15%; the official page omits the percent sign after 15)
- APIs and Connectors (15–25%)
- Troubleshooting (15–25%)
- Product baseline: FortiSOAR 7.6

No detailed objectives or contract were published on the FortiRecon or FortiDeceptor placeholder pages as of the check date.
