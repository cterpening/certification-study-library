Fortinet NSE 5 in Cloud Security certification-path snapshot
Track source: https://training.fortinet.com/local/staticpage/view.php?page=nse_5_cloud_security

Certification requirement
- Hold active NSE 4 FortiOS.
- Pass one proctored NSE 5 Cloud Security exam within 2 years.
- Certification is active for 2 years from completion of the second requirement.

3. Fortinet NSE 5 - FortiADC Administrator
   - Track-page status: announced for Q3 2026
