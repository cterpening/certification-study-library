Exam MB-330: Microsoft Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management Functional Consultant
Official objective baseline: Skills measured as of June 20, 2025
Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/mb-330
Captured: 2026-09-01

Skills at a glance

Implement product information management (25–30%)
Implement inventory and asset management (20–25%)
Implement and manage supply chain processes (15–20%)
Implement warehouse management and transportation management (20–25%)
Implement master planning (10–15%)

Implement product information management (25–30%)

Create and manage products
- Create and release products and variants
- Create and apply product templates
- Create BOMs and BOM versions
- Create and configure category hierarchies
- Create and manage product lifecycle states
- Create and manage product attributes

Configure prerequisites for products
- Configure storage, tracking, and product dimension groups
- Configure item model groups
- Configure reservation hierarchies
- Configure unit sequence groups

Configure additional product details
- Create and assign bar codes and GTIN codes
- Configure product unit conversions
- Configure default order settings
- Configure product filter codes for warehouse operations

Manage inventory pricing and costing
- Configure and maintain costing versions for standard and planned costs
- Configure price groups and standard-cost items
- Configure default item, purchase, and sales prices and trade-agreement discounts

Implement inventory and asset management (20–25%)

Manage and process inventory activities
- Create and process BOM, item arrival, output, transfer, movement, adjustment, and counting journals
- Create and process transfer orders
- Configure and manage inventory closing and adjustment
- Apply and remove inventory blocking
- Create batch disposition codes
- Configure and run inventory reports including ABC classification

Manage quality
- Configure and process nonconformances
- Configure quality associations and quality-control components
- Process quality and quarantine orders

Configure and implement asset management
- Create and configure assets, asset types, lifecycle models, and states
- Create/manage work orders, settings, capacity, and loans
- Schedule preventive and reactive maintenance
- Configure maintenance plans and functional locations
- Manage maintenance requests, work-order consumption, and scheduling

Implement and manage supply chain processes (15–20%)

Implement procurement and sourcing
- Create vendors, requisitions, RFQs, POs, purchase agreements, and vendor rebates
- Configure consignment, over/under deliveries, schedules, categories, charges, policies, collaboration, returns, registration, receipt, and workflows

Implement landed cost
- Configure parameters and voyage statuses
- Process voyages with PO or transfer orders
- Configure estimation, tracking, tolerances, journey templates, cost types, and auto-costs
- Track inbound voyages and container journeys

Implement common sales and marketing features
- Create customers, agreements, quotations, orders, reservations, returns, groups, and commissions
- Configure intercompany orders, rebates, delivery schedules, ATP, CTP, and product bundles

Implement warehouse management and transportation management (20–25%)

Configure and perform warehouse management
- Configure sites, warehouses, locations, formats, profiles, types, zones, work policies/breaks, directives, wave/load/work templates, labels, packing dimensions, receiving, shipping, and inventory status
- Configure replenishment, load planning, inventory movement/work, cycle counting, mobile inbound/outbound, containerization, packaging, and cross docking

Implement transportation management
- Configure carriers/groups, loads/shipments, freight bills/invoices, routes/guides, rate/route engines, dock appointments, and freight reconciliation

Implement the Warehouse Management mobile app
- Configure menus, menu items, display settings, and detours
- Install/configure the app and warehouse workers

Implement master planning (10–15%)

Configure and manage master planning
- Configure coverage groups/item coverage, parameters/plans, positive/negative days, action messages, delay, time fences, period templates, and safety margins
- Run plans and analyze results
- Evaluate/firm planned purchase and transfer orders
- Use Supply Schedule
- Configure and process safety-stock journals

Change log summary

The June 20, 2025 version increases product and warehouse/transport weights,
decreases inventory/asset weight, removes the prior inventory-configuration
group, revises multiple groups, and adds a Warehouse Management mobile-app group.
