Description
The NSE 6 in SASE certification validates your ability to deploy, manage, and monitor advanced endpoint protection and data security features using FortiClient EMS, FortiEDR, and FortiDLP solutions. The certification exams cover advanced operational tasks associated with using FortiClient EMS, FortiEDR, and FortiDLP solutions, including endpoint posture enforcement, threat detection and response, and data loss prevention.
Who Should Attempt the Certification?
We recommend this certification for cybersecurity professionals who require the expertise needed to design, manage, support, and analyze advanced Fortinet SASE solutions.
