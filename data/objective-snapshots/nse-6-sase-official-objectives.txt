NSE 6 in SASE
Official certification page checked: 2026-09-02
https://training.fortinet.com/local/staticpage/view.php?page=nse_6_sase

Description
The certification validates the ability to deploy, manage, and monitor advanced endpoint protection and data security features using FortiClient EMS, FortiEDR, and FortiDLP. The page identifies endpoint posture enforcement, threat detection and response, and data loss prevention.

Program requirements
- Hold the NSE 4 FortiOS certification.
- Pass one proctored NSE 6 SASE exam within 2 years.
- Certification is active for 2 years from the second exam.

Published option exams
- Fortinet NSE 6 - FortiClient EMS Administrator
- Fortinet NSE 6 - FortiEDR Administrator
- Fortinet NSE 6 - FortiDLP Administrator

FortiClient EMS Administrator published objective groups
- FortiClient EMS design and deployment
- FortiClient provisioning and deployment
- Implement endpoint security
- Zero trust and Security Fabric integration
- Troubleshooting
- Product baseline: FortiClient EMS 7.4, FortiGate 7.6, FortiClient 7.4

FortiEDR Administrator published objective groups
- FortiEDR system
- FortiEDR security settings and policies
- Events, forensics, and threat hunting
- FortiEDR integration
- FortiEDR troubleshooting
- Product baseline: FortiEDR 7.0

FortiDLP Administrator published domains
- FortiDLP fundamentals and deployment (25–35%)
- Data identification and enforcement (20–30%)
- Detection and investigation (15–25%)
- Troubleshooting (15–25%)
- Product baseline: FortiDLP 26
