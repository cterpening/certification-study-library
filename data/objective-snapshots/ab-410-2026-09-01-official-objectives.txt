Audience profile
As a candidate for this Microsoft Certification, you’re a professional who
builds AI-powered solutions in Microsoft Power Platform by using Microsoft
Copilot, natural language prompts, and low-code tools. You create apps, data
models, and flows that connect to agents, AI models and prompts, and
visualizations to enrich these experiences.
In this role, your responsibilities include:
Developing Dataverse data models, model-driven apps, and canvas apps.
Integrating agents and Copilot features into canvas apps, model-driven apps,
and Power Pages sites.
Creating cloud flows and business logic.
You collaborate with environment and security administrators; governance
teams; agent developers, solution architects, and other app builders; and
business stakeholders.
